5. Sensitivity Analysis

Lab 11 tested how changes in important assumptions affected ADM's projected financial statements, cash flow, liquidity, and valuation.

Gross Margin Sensitivity

My base gross margin assumption was 6.75%.

When gross margin was reduced to 6.25%, 2030E operating profit declined to approximately $683.78 million, a decrease of approximately $155.09 million from the base case.

The lower gross margin also increased ADM's financing requirements. By 2030E, the ending liquidity facility balance reached approximately $6,229.3 million.

This exceeded my $6 billion facility limit, so the scenario failed the model's liquidity validation.

Because the scenario failed, I reported the valuation as UNAVAILABLE rather than producing a value from a scenario that violated my model constraint.

The causal path was:

Lower Gross Margin → Lower Gross Profit → Lower Operating Profit → Greater Financing Need → Facility Limit Exceeded → Valuation Unavailable

Capital Expenditure Sensitivity

I also tested annual CapEx of $1,600 million, compared with the $1,400 million base assumption.

Under the $1,600 million CapEx scenario, the ending liquidity facility balance reached approximately $6,539.8 million by 2030E.

This also exceeded the $6 billion facility limit, so the scenario failed validation and its valuation was reported as UNAVAILABLE.

The causal path was:

Higher CapEx → Greater Cash Outflow → Greater Financing Need → Facility Limit Exceeded → Valuation Unavailable

Sensitivity Interpretation

The sensitivity analysis shows which assumptions had the largest effects over the ranges I tested. It does not establish which scenario is most likely to occur.

The results also show why liquidity is an important limitation in my model. Changes that reduce profitability or increase cash requirements can cause the financing facility to exceed the $6 billion limit.

6. Interpretation

My base pro-forma estimates ADM at $33.70 per share, compared with a market price of $82.42 per share on September 24, 2026.

My sensitivity analysis also showed that the model can become constrained by liquidity when operating performance weakens or cash requirements increase.

The assumption I would investigate next is gross margin. Gross margin directly affects ADM's operating profitability, cash generation, financing requirements, and ultimately valuation. Additional research into sustainable long-run margins would help determine whether my 6.75% base assumption is reasonable.
